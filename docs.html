the idea

pair any meme to any creator. the meme becomes a coin on pump.fun, and the coin's creator fees go to the creator you picked, as a gift inside their own app. they don't need a wallet, an account or a claim. every other pad says only the creator can claim. this one has nothing to claim.

three apps, three rails: X → X Money, Instagram → gifts on their reels, Facebook → stars.

a pairing

  1. you pick the app, type the handle, pick a meme and a name. the site checks the handle's shape and shows you the vault it will pay.
  2. your browser makes the coin's own key. it never leaves the page.
  3. the server builds one transaction: pump.fun's create_v2 with the coin's creator set to that creator's vault, your optional first buy, a memo meme:pair:1:…, and a zero-SOL transfer to the tag so the pairing can be found later. it simulates it on Solana first.
  4. your wallet signs and pays. the coin's key signs last, in your browser. the server checks every signature, then sends it.

the coin's description reads Paired to X @handle via .meme. it can't be edited after launch, and nobody, including .meme, can point the coin's fees anywhere else.

you sign one thinga pump.fun launch where you are the payer and the buyer, and the creator is their vault. your wallet shows the cost before you approve.

the vault

every creator has one vault per app: a Solana address derived from a secret only this site holds, HMAC-SHA256(secret, "meme:vault:app:handle"). the same handle always lands on the same vault, so every meme paired to them pays into one place, and the vault exists before any coin does.

pump.fun sends each coin's creator fees to its vault. the vault never signs a launch. its key is used for one thing: moving its fees to the payout wallet when a gift is due.

the trust holethe site holds the key that moves fees out of a vault, and a person sends the gifts. if they stopped, nothing would hide it: the creator's waiting balance would keep growing on their page, and their receipts would stop.

the gift

each time a vault holds $25 or more, the payout wallet collects it, and a person sends exactly $25 inside the creator's app. same size every time, so every receipt reads the same. the rest waits for the next one.

  • X → X Money. sent to their X account. X Money is US-only for now and allows one payment per person per day.
  • Instagram → gifts. sent as gifts on their latest reel. only works once the creator has gifts turned on.
  • Facebook → stars. sent as stars on their latest video or reel. only works once the creator can receive stars.

a person sends each one because none of these apps lets software send a gift. until a creator's app can receive, their $25s wait in their vault, in public. nothing is ever re-pointed to anyone else.

receipts

every gift is written on Solana by the payout wallet as a memo, with a zero-SOL transfer to the tag:

meme:paid:1:p=x&h=handle&usd=25&sol=0.17&proof=https://…

only memos signed by the payout wallet are counted. to check one: open its transaction, read the memo, open the proof link on the creator's post and match the time.

the cut

pump.fun charges its own fee on every trade; part of it is the creator fee, and that part is what lands in the vault. .meme takes nothing from a vault. what is collected from a vault is spent on that creator's gifts.

the apps keep part of what is spent on a gift, and the network charges a few cents to move it. every receipt prints the two numbers that show it: the SOL that went out, and the dollars that arrived.

consent

  • nobody asked them first. being paired means someone made a meme and pointed its fees at you. it doesn't mean you know .meme exists.
  • a gift is not an endorsement. nothing on this site says a creator supports a coin.
  • one message ends it. a creator who asks out is out for good: no new coin can pair to them. what already waits is sent to them only if they want it; otherwise it stays in their vault, published, never re-pointed.
  • the launcher is public. every pairing shows the wallet that launched it.
  • no faces, no names in our art. handles only appear where a visitor typed them.

words

pairing line

the line in a coin that says whose meme it is. locked at launch.

vault

the address that is the coin's on-chain creator. its fees land there.

queue

what is waiting in a vault right now, in public.

quantum

$25. every payout is exactly one of these.

landing

the moment a gift shows up on their post.

cold

a creator whose app can't receive yet. their queue waits.

receipt

the on-chain record of a landing, with its proof.

out

a creator who asked to stop. nothing new pairs to them.

a dash

means there is no number to show. a zero means we checked and found none.

tag

the address every pairing and receipt touches, so the chain can be searched.

not proven yet

what is built and what has been seen working are different things. today:

  • the launch is built with pump.fun's own SDK and simulated on Solana before you sign, but the first real launch through this site is the first proof.
  • after a coin graduates to PumpSwap, its creator fees may arrive as wrapped SOL. collecting them is built but not yet tested with a real coin.
  • X Money is US-only and allows one payment per person per day. gifts and stars depend on what each creator has turned on.
  • a gift is sent by a person, so the only proof it landed is the link on its receipt.
