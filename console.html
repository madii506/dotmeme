the payout desk

collect. send. record.

the desk for the payout wallet. it moves a creator's fees out of their vault, and writes each gift on-chain with its proof. anyone can watch; only the payout wallet can sign.

01collect

when a vault holds $25 or more, collect it. the fees move to the payout wallet in one transaction, with a memo.

02send

from the payout phone, send $25 inside their app: X Money on X, gifts on their latest reel, stars on Facebook.

03record

write the receipt below: what arrived, the SOL it took, and the link to the gift on their post.
