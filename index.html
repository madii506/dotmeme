06 words

the whole thing in eight words.

pairing line

the one line in a coin that says whose meme it is. locked at launch.

vault

the wallet that is the coin's on-chain creator. its fees land there.

queue

what is waiting in a vault right now, in public.

quantum

$25. every payout is exactly one of these.

landing

the moment a gift shows up on their post.

cold

a creator whose app can't receive yet. their queue waits.

receipt

the on-chain record of a landing, with its proof.

out

a creator who asked to stop. nothing new pairs to them.

08 consent

nobody asked them first. one message ends it.

a gift is not an endorsement

being paired means someone made a meme and pointed its fees at you. it doesn't mean you know .meme exists, and nothing here says otherwise.

one message, for good

a creator who asks out is out: no new coin can pair to them, and what is already waiting is sent to them only if they want it. otherwise it stays in their vault, published, never re-pointed.

no faces, no names in our art

handles only appear where a visitor typed them. every illustration on this site uses blank handles.

the launcher is public

every pairing shows the wallet that launched it. a meme is somebody's choice, and that somebody is on the record.
